What does Originality Desk compare?

It compares the textual content of your submission against documents you select. It does not claim to search the open web, subscription databases, or all published scholarship.

Is an overlap score a plagiarism verdict?

No. Shared terminology, quotations, and properly attributed material can overlap. Examine the passages and citation context before making a judgment.

What happens to uploaded files?

Files and reports are handled by the application server and appear in your workspace. This interface does not establish a retention, encryption, or regulatory-compliance guarantee. Consult your server operator before uploading sensitive work. You can request document deletion from the Documents view.

Are AI-authorship, semantic matching, or OCR available?

No. AI-authorship detection, semantic analysis, and OCR are unavailable in this interface. Image-only or scanned documents may not yield extractable text.

Can I invite collaborators or recover a password by email?

Workspace invitations, email password reset, and multi-factor authentication are unavailable. Contact your deployment administrator for account access help.
